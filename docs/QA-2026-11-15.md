improvements and fixes needed." Mode agreed up front: report, and fix what is

## How

- **E2E:** Playwright smoke + offline (69 tests) and the quality gates (32
  tests: tap targets, type floor, light/dark, 360 and 390 px).

  probe also run on its own so one failure cannot hide the rest. Catalog
  sweep: tables without RLS, RLS without policies, SECURITY DEFINER without a
  `search_path`, functions anon can execute, `using (true)` policies.
- **Screens with data, per role (new in this pass):** a local PostgREST on
  the harness database, so every read went through the real policies, seeded
  with a family, 35 itinerary days in two countries, 42 expenses, 30 photos,
  two albums, jar quotes, a country card, stories, chat. Signed in as a
  parent, a kid, a guest and nobody, a script opened 60 screens at 390 px,
  light and dark, and recorded: failed requests, console errors, tap targets
  under 44 px, text under 12 px, controls without a name, sideways scroll.
  Then the new flows by hand (jar form, albums, cash wallet, insights), and
  every screen again with the network off behind the real service worker.
- **Security review by reading:** migrations 00087-00093, the new edge
  function, the join flow, headers, dependencies, secrets, unsafe HTML.
- **Live (Vercel):** production deployment state, 7 days of runtime errors.
- **Live (Supabase): not done.** The connector failed to connect in this

## Found, not fixed - needs a decision

Ordered by what I would do first.

### Security and privacy

- **S1 (Medium) - the join page is now one tap from the login screen, for
  anyone.** Since 00093 the login screen hands the join token to whoever opens
  the app's address. The database side is sound (asking grants nothing, probe
  93). What changed is who can reach the page and the request queue:
  - The join page's welcome text names the family and the children by first
    name. Before 00093 only people who got the WhatsApp link read it; now it
    is public to anyone who finds the address. Suggest: no children's names
    in that text.
  - Approval rests on a name the requester typed. Someone can ask as "סבתא
    רותי" with their own address. The parents' screen does show the address
    and "דרך Google" / typed, which is the right information - but "אישור
    כולם" approves every waiting request in one tap. Suggest: "approve all"
    skips, or asks again for, requests whose address was typed rather than
    proven by Google.
  - Anyone can send 30 requests an hour, each one a push to both parents, and
    at 200 waiting requests the function answers "busy" to everyone, real
    guests included. A typed request can also overwrite the name and kids'
    names of someone else's waiting request by reusing their address.
    Suggest: from the open login screen offer Google only (a proven address),
    and keep the typed-address form for the printed QR / WhatsApp link.
- **S2 (Medium-Low) - CSP is still report-only**, as shipped in August. It
  reports and blocks nothing. The policy has now been through three months
  of real use; worth one session of reading the browser's reports on the
  map, photos and recommend screens and switching the header to enforcing.
- **S3 (Low) - AI functions write to "the active trip", not the caller's.**
  `country-profile-generate` (new) checks the caller is a parent, then writes
  with the service role to whichever trip has `is_active`, as `facts-generate`
  and four others do. Correct with one family. If a second trip is ever
  active, a parent of one could overwrite the other's cards. Suggest: resolve
  the trip from the caller's member row.
- **S4 (Low) - deleting an album can leave its files in storage.**
  `deleteAlbum` reads the photos' paths, deletes the album (rows cascade),
  then removes the files. If the first read fails the album still goes and
  the files stay, unreachable from the app but not deleted. Suggest: stop if
  the paths could not be read.
- **S5 (Accepted, unchanged)** - `xlsx` advisories with no npm fix; runs only
  in a parent's browser on a file they pick (SECURITY-CHECKS 15/08).

### Performance

- **P1 (Medium) - the home screen makes 47 database requests for a parent**
  on a cold open (37 for a kid, 35 for a guest); every other screen 9-24.
  Several are asked twice in the same load: the member row (on almost every
  screen), the four "waiting for approval" counts, today's itinerary day. A
  guest's home also asks for bookings and itinerary days, which a guest's
  policies never return. Each request pays the Asia-Europe round trip (the
  09/11 finding), so the count matters more than the query time. Suggest:
  one RPC for the home screen and one for the bottom bar's badges, and a
  single shared member lookup. X I measured counts, not time: the local
  stack has no network distance.

### Offline

- **O1 (Low) - a kid's route screen does not open offline** ("could not load
  the route"), while a parent's does and the kid's home does. The jar has no
  device copy for anyone. Neither is on the offline-critical list (rule 6)
  and both say so correctly, but kids are the ones who open these in a car
  or on a plane. The country card already keeps a device copy for that
  reason.
- **O2 (Low) - the offline fallback page shows the parents' bottom bar** to
  whoever lands on it, kid or guest included.
- **O3 (Low) - the shell cache keeps every page ever opened**, including each
  /s/<slug> and /join/<token>, and is never trimmed (the asset cache is).

### UX / UI

- **U1 (Low) - the capture button sits bottom-centre, over content, until
  the page is scrolled:** the label of "העלאת מסמך" on documents, the "כאן"
  marker and the "קפיצה להיום" pill on the itinerary, day chips on recommend
  (that row does not scroll clear). Fixed where it blocked a control (map,
  budget). A lasting answer is a decision: the button in the corner rather
  than the centre, or hidden on screens that have their own primary action.
- **U2 (Low) - emergency screen assumes "parent" until the member loads**
  (`!member`), so the edit buttons can flash for a kid on a slow line. The
  no-backend shell relies on that default; it needs a third state, not a
  one-line change.
- **U3 (Low, latent) - /pocket is on the parents-only list** while the kids'
  bar gains a "דמי כיס" tab when the `pocketMoney` flag is switched on. The
  flag is off; turning it on as is would send a kid to "reserved for parents"
  from their own tab.
- **U4 (Low, accessibility)** - the map screen has no page heading (h1); the
  capture button's hidden file input has no label (on every parent screen).

### Process

- **Q1 - the quality gates cover 8 screens with no data.** Everything found
  above was on screens with real rows, or on screens added since the gate
  list was written (jar, albums, facts). The per-role crawl used here is a
  PostgREST container, a 40-line proxy and a seed file; kept as a script it
  would let every QA pass see what a kid and a guest see.
- **Q2 - `run.sh` stops at the first failing probe.** That is how finding 1
  hid seven probe files. It should run them all and fail at the end.
- **Q3 - `docs/SCHEMA.sql` does not mention 21 of the 51 tables** (among them
  jar_quotes, country_profiles, place_options, comments, reactions,
  guest_join_requests). CLAUDE.md names it as the reference for rule 1.
