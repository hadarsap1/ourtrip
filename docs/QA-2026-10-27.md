merged since the 17/10 pass (#124-#154: guest groups, wall replies, preview

## How

- **Code:** typecheck, lint, 637 unit tests (also under TZ=Asia/Jerusalem,
  Ho_Chi_Minh, Tokyo, Tbilisi), production build.
- **Database:** all 74 migrations on a fresh Postgres 16 + the RLS harness
  (`scripts/rls/run.sh`): 364 probes, 0 failures. Schema-wide sweep: every
  public table has RLS and at least one policy; every SECURITY DEFINER
  function pins `search_path`; no `USING (true)` policy anywhere.
- **E2E:** Playwright smoke + offline suite (70 tests) and quality gates
  (32 tests: tap targets, type floor, light/dark, 360 and 390 px) against the
  production build.
- **Live (read-only):** Supabase advisors, migration history, cron history,
  backups, edge function versions, 24h logs (API, Postgres, functions), the
  trip's real data.
- **Repo vs. production:** fingerprint of every policy (public + storage) and
  every public function, local vs. live. Policies identical on all 44 tables
  plus storage; functions identical once whitespace and comments are ignored.
  `visa_requirements` has an explicit `WITH CHECK` live that equals its
  `USING` - same meaning. Edge function deploy times all postdate their last
  repo change.
- **Code review** of the high-risk changes: `preview-session` (mints real
  guest/kid sessions), group audiences 00063-00066, rule 3 on every path a
  guest or the public can reach a photo, the join link.

## Found and fixed

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High (rule 3) | **A public story put photos on the open web that guests may not see.** In the composer, a parent who picks "public" and unticks "share the photos too" still got every photo copied to the public bucket (`syncPublicCopies` checked only the group audience), so a kid photo never approved or never shared was readable by anyone with the `/s/` link. Live: no public story yet, 0 public copies, nothing leaked. | Copies only `approved AND shared_with_guests AND audience = []` (`photoMayGoPublic`, tested). |
| 2 | Medium (rule 3) | **Taking a photo back did not take it off the open page.** Unsharing, rejecting, narrowing a photo to some groups or deleting it left its public copy and the story kept showing it. | `withdrawPublicCopies` runs on each of those (before the delete, since the links cascade). |
| 3 | Medium | **Itinerary import: a text date ("3 Nov 2026") landed a day early** in Israel and every trip country. `Date.parse` reads local midnight, `toISOString` shifted it to the previous UTC day. Numeric and Excel dates were fine. | Local calendar date; test runs under three UTC+ zones and fails without the fix. |
| 4 | Low | **Gmail booking import searched a window one day off** before 07:00 in Asia (same UTC slicing). | Local calendar date. |
